This lets the platform send invitations, event access codes, and password resets from noreply@606athletics.com. You'll paste three records into GoDaddy — that's the whole job. It cannot break your website or any existing email.

  3. Find 606athletics.com → click DNS (or ⋯ → Manage DNS).
Only add records. Don't delete or change anything already in the list — the three below are all brand-new entries.

Quick answers

Will this affect my website or current email?
No. These records live on their own corners of the domain ("send." and "resend._domainkey.") that nothing else uses. Your website and any existing email keep working exactly as before.
What are these records, in plain English?
They prove to Gmail, Outlook, etc. that 60'6" ID is genuinely allowed to send email for 606athletics.com — a cryptographic signature, a return address for bounces, and a sending permission note. Without them, providers would junk the messages.
GoDaddy says changes can take 48 hours — really?
Almost always minutes. The 48-hour warning is a worst-case boilerplate.
GoDaddy put quotes around my TXT value
That's normal — leave them. Just don't type extra quotes yourself.
