Getting started

Three things to know before your first sign-in.

Sign in
  • You'll receive an invitation email from your organization. Open the link, set a password, and you're in. There is nothing to download — it works in the browser on any phone, tablet, or computer.
  • Guest coaches at an event may instead receive a 6-character access code — enter it on the app's Redeem page with your email.
  • Forgot your password? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page.
First load of the day can take ~30–60 seconds. The app server naps when nobody is using it. Wait once and it stays fast. On camp days, open the app before athletes arrive so it's already awake.

What you see depends on who you are

Same athletes, same data — a different home screen and menu built around your job. Tap your role.

🏟️  Organization Owner / Admin — Organization HQ

You land on: Organization HQ — athletes, teams, coaches, evaluations awaiting review, upcoming events, and grad-class shortcuts. Every number is a button: tap Awaiting Review to open the review queue, tap a team to open that team.

Your menu: Overview · Teams · Athletes · Development · Events — with everything else (Reports, Scout, Staff, Templates, Drills, Audit Log, Settings) under Administration.

You can:

  • Run events end to end — roster, check-in with bib numbers, stations, evaluator assignments, live progress
  • Give a guest coach temporary access that expires when the event ends
  • Manage staff, evaluation templates (by age band and position), and the drill catalog
  • See every report, export CSVs, and generate player PDFs with charts
  • Set your organization's logo (Settings) — it appears beside "Powered by 60'6" ID"
🧢  Coach — Coach Hub

You land on: My Athletes — active development goals first, today's event actions, and your athletes' recent progress.

Your menu: Overview · My Teams · My Athletes · Evaluations · Development.

You can:

  • Check athletes in at events and score at stations you're assigned to
  • Log verified measurements (exit velo, 60-yard, pop time…) — a new personal best automatically celebrates and notifies the athlete
  • Create development goals with a target and a follow-up date
  • Write notes with a visibility level (general, development, private staff, parent-visible…) — notes are never overwritten or lost
  • Upload photos/video tied to an athlete, an event, or an evaluation
📋  Evaluator — Evaluation Mode

You land on: your assignments — station, group, how many players are left, and one big button: Continue Evaluating. No admin screens, nothing to get lost in.

Your menu: Today's Event · Evaluate · Submitted.

The field loop: pick a player (search by name or bib number) → score with big tap targets → add a quick note or photo → it saves automatically → Next player. The player's photo, name, and bib stay pinned at the top; the app warns you before you submit with required scores missing.

Weak wifi? Keep scoring. Scores save on your device and sync when the connection returns — the status pill tells you the truth (Synced vs Waiting). Don't clear your browser data mid-event; that's where offline work lives.

🔭  Head Scout — Scout Mode + Review

You land on: the Review Desk — evaluations awaiting your approval, who's improving, top movers, and flagged players.

Your menu: Overview · Review · Discover · Watchlist · Compare · Events.

You can:

  • Approve or return submitted evaluations (returns go back to the evaluator with your note)
  • Work the Prospect Board — filter by grad class and position, see verified metrics with trust badges
  • ⭐ Star prospects to your private Watchlist
  • Compare up to four players side by side with charts
  • Open any evaluation's visual results summary — score, change, top strengths and improvement areas
⚾  Athlete / Parent — My Development

You land on: My Development — your development headline (e.g. "↑ +14% development this season"), your top 3 current priorities from your coach, personal bests with verification badges, and what's next.

Your permanent 60'6" ID (like 606-A337F768) stays with you forever — across seasons, teams, and organizations. Every season stacks under the same profile; nothing is ever erased.

You can:

  • Complete your profile (photo, height/weight) — the app shows what's missing
  • See every evaluation result, measurement, and milestone
  • Turn on your Public ID Story — a shareable page (with QR) showing only approved content
  • Parents: approve or reject photos/videos of your child before anything is displayed — nothing under-13 is ever shown without your approval

How-to guides

The most common jobs, step by step.

Run a camp / evaluation day (site manager)
  1. Open the app before athletes arrive (wakes the server, caches templates).
  2. Events → your event → set status to Evaluation Active.
  3. Check-In tab: check athletes in, assign bib numbers and groups.
  4. Confirm Stations and Evaluators assignments.
  5. Watch the Live Progress tab: checked-in, in progress, complete, missing scores, videos awaiting approval. Tap any player to see exactly which metrics are missing.
  6. After the event: Review Queue → approve → Reports for rankings, CSV, and player PDFs.

Print blank paper score sheets as backup. Dead battery beats no plan.

Give a guest coach access for one event
  1. Event → Evaluators tab → enter the coach's personal email.
  2. Pick their station / player group.
  3. The app emails them a secure 6-character code (it's also shown on screen so you can text it).
  4. They sign in via the Redeem page with that code — and see only their assigned players and station. No parent contacts, no medical info, no other coaches' private notes.
  5. Access expires automatically when the event ends — or revoke it instantly from the same tab.
Score players at the field (evaluator)
  1. Sign in → tap Continue Evaluating on your assignment.
  2. Find the player — type their bib number (fastest) or name.
  3. Score each category with the big buttons. Only categories relevant to that player's age and position appear.
  4. Add a quick note or snap a photo/video right from the form if asked to.
  5. Watch the pill say Synced. If you're offline it says so — keep going, it syncs later.
  6. Fix anything the missing-scores warning flags, then Submit and tap Next (it shows the next player's bib and name).
Approve or return evaluations (head scout)
  1. Review → open a submitted evaluation.
  2. Tap Results summary for the visual version — score, change, radar, top strengths and improvement areas.
  3. Approve — it becomes part of the athlete's permanent record and feeds reports.
  4. Or Return with a note — the evaluator sees it, fixes it, and resubmits.
Track development & set goals (coach)
  1. My Athletes → open the athlete → Development Goals.
  2. Create a goal: what needs improvement, the recommended action, target date, and a follow-up evaluation date.
  3. Update progress as they work — athletes see their top 3 priorities on their own screen (never a wall of weaknesses).
  4. Log verified measurements on the Verified tab — the athlete's charts compare each number against their previous, their personal best, and age/position benchmarks.
Complete a profile & share a Public Story (athlete / parent)
  1. My Development → Edit → add a photo and bio. Under-18 photos wait for approval before they're shown anywhere.
  2. The profile meter shows exactly what's missing (photo, height, weight, recent evaluation, approved video).
  3. Toggle Public ID Story to get a shareable link + QR. It shows only approved evaluations, verified measurements, and approved media — nothing private, ever.

Common questions

Quick answers to the things people actually ask.

The app is stuck loading / the first login is slow

The server sleeps after ~15 quiet minutes and takes 30–60 seconds to wake. Wait once — everything after that is fast. On event days, open the app before athletes arrive.

It says "Too many attempts. Please wait a minute."

A safety limit: about 15 sign-ins per minute from one network. At an event, have people sign in as they arrive rather than everyone at once. Waiting 60 seconds clears it.

What happens to my scores if the wifi dies mid-evaluation?

Nothing is lost. Scores save to the device instantly and sync when connection returns — the status pill only says Synced when the server has truly confirmed it. Even closing the browser and reopening it offline keeps your work. The one thing to never do mid-event: clear the browser's site data — that's where offline work lives.

Are photos and videos of kids safe here?

Yes — by design. Every file records who uploaded it and its consent status. Media of athletes under 13 is never displayed anywhere until a guardian approves it. Files marked private are staff-only regardless of consent. The public story page shows approved content only, and files are stored in private cloud storage that only the app can serve.

What does the "Verified" badge on a measurement mean?

Every number shows where it came from: Athlete Submitted Parent Submitted Coach Submitted Event Verified Device Verified 60'6" Verified

Self-reported numbers look visibly different from staff-verified ones — a college coach looking at "88 mph, Event Verified" knows a staff member measured it at a real event. The app enforces this: an athlete literally cannot mark their own number as verified.

My athlete changed teams / a new season started. Do we make a new profile?

No — never. That's the whole point of the permanent 60'6" ID. Each season stacks under the same profile with its own team, measurements, and evaluations, and the Career Overview shows the multi-year story. Old evaluations are never erased or overwritten.

Why can't I see certain information (parent contacts, private notes)?

Access is role-based on purpose. Evaluators see only their assigned players and never see family contacts, medical, or financial details. Coaches don't see other coaches' private notes. Scout assessments marked confidential are visible only to organization leadership. If you think you're missing something you should have, ask your organization's admin — they control roles.

Can I use it on my phone? Is there an app to install?

It's built phone-first — evaluators score on phones at the field. Nothing to install: open the app in your browser. Tip: use your browser's Add to Home Screen to get an app-style icon.

I didn't get my invitation email

Check spam first. Ask your organization admin to re-send the invite (Players → your athlete → Invite, or Staff → Invite for coaches). For event access codes, the site manager can also read the code off their screen and text it to you.

Who owns the data? What happens between camps?

Your organization's data belongs to your organization, in its own walled-off space — organizations can never see each other's athletes. Everything persists between events: run a camp in August and another in October, and every athlete's history simply grows. Evaluations are permanent, timestamped records.

Something looks wrong or broke — what do I do?

Note what you were doing, your role, and the screen you were on, then tell your organization admin. A hard refresh (pull down on mobile) fixes most display oddities after an update. Your scores are safe either way — saving is the first thing the app does.
