Every camp. Every rep. One platform.

A guided run-through for demoing 60'6" ID to coaches — what to click, what to say, and the moments that land. App: 606-scout.surge.sh

⏱ 15 minutes before the call

  • Wake the server: open the app and sign in once — first load after idle takes 30–60 seconds; every load after is instant.
  • Sign in ahead of time on two devices: laptop as owner@pbgscout.com, phone as eval1@pbgscout.com (password for all demo accounts: Scout2025!). Signing in early also avoids the 15-logins/minute safety limit mid-demo.
  • Download the demo roster to the laptop: demo-roster.csv — it's crafted so the import wizard shows off every safeguard live.
  • If sharing your phone screen isn't practical, resize the browser window narrow — the whole app is phone-first.

1Open cold — the landing page (1 min)

Do: open 606-scout.surge.sh logged out.

"60'6" ID gives every athlete one permanent player profile — every evaluation, verified measurement, video, and season of development, in one place, forever."

2Sign in → "Where are you working today?" (1 min)

Do: sign in as the owner → the workspace chooser appears (if it goes straight to a dashboard, use the Workspace switcher in the sidebar).

"Same platform, different lenses. An owner runs the organization; a coach sees their athletes; an evaluator sees only today's assignment. One login, no clutter."

Do: pick Organization HQ.

3Organization HQ (2 min)

Do: point at the KPI cards — athletes, teams, coaches, awaiting review, upcoming events. Click Teams → open a team → back. Click a Class of… in the grad dropdown → the roster filters instantly.

"Every number is a button. Two taps from 'how many athletes do we have' to any kid's complete history."

4★ The centerpiece — run a camp from scratch (6 min)

Create the event

Do: Events → New Event → name it Fall Evaluation Demo, today's date, any location → create. Point out the setup checklist on the Overview tab: Roster → Groups → Stations → Evaluators → Activated.

Import the roster (the wow moment)

Do: Roster tab → Import Roster CSV → upload demo-roster.csv → walk the preview:

Stations & staff

Do: Stations tab → Add preset stations → tick Athletic Movement, Hitting, Infield, Pitching → add. Evaluators tab → the 1-2-3 stepper: pick an evaluator → group → station → Save.

"Stations come from our 60'6" evaluation standard — and the right form finds the right kid automatically. A 9-year-old and a 17-year-old pitcher get different, age-appropriate evaluations at the same station. Nobody builds forms at the field."

Do: set status → Evaluation Active.

5★ The phone — what evaluators feel (4 min)

Do: switch to the phone (signed in as eval1). It opens on today's assignment: station, group, "N remaining", one big button.

"An evaluator never navigates. Sign in → my assignment → start."

Do: open the demo camp assignment → search a bib number → open an athlete → tap big 1–5 scores → watch the pill flip to Synced → show the #next-athlete button in the footer → Submit one.

"Autosave on every tap — and if the ballpark wifi dies, they keep scoring. Everything syncs when it comes back. We've torture-tested exactly that."

6The manager's view — Live Progress (2 min)

Do: laptop → the seeded PBG Midwest Spring Evaluation Camp → Live Progress tab (it has rich data). Show checked-in / in-progress / complete / missing, station and evaluator completion → click a player → exactly which scores are missing.

"The site manager sees the whole camp breathing — and can find the one kid with a missing score without asking anyone."

7Review → Results (2 min)

Do: Review queue → open a submitted evaluation → Results summary: score, change, top strengths/improvements, radar → then Approve one.

"Evaluators score, but a head scout approves — quality control before anything touches a kid's permanent record."

8The payoff — the athlete (3 min)

Do: Players → open Miguel Reyes → the digital player card: photo, 2037 | 3B | R/L, permanent ID, six KPIs, verified exit velo. Show the Seasons panel (two seasons, one profile) and the Player Story timeline.

"This is the product parents care about: one permanent 60'6" ID that follows the athlete across seasons, teams — their whole story, verified."

Do: optionally sign in as athlete.demo@pbgscout.com — the athlete sees "+14% development this season" and their top-3 priorities.

"For the athlete it leads with development, not rankings — what am I working on, and am I getting better."

9Close (1 min)

Do: flash Scout Mode (prospect board, ⭐ watchlist, compare) and Reports (insight cards, PDF with charts).

"Everything you saw is live today — running on phones at a real camp this month. Next up on the roadmap: configurable athletic testing, equipment scheduling, and staffing recommendations sized to each event."

If something wobbles

Slow first screen: the server was asleep — it's the free hosting tier, not the app. Talking point: "production hosting removes this."
"Too many attempts" on login: the security rate-limit. Wait 60 seconds — or avoid it entirely by signing in before the call.
Wrong workspace after login: use the Workspace switcher in the sidebar, under the organization name.
Made a mess during the demo? The demo event you created can be deleted afterward with the trash button on the event header. Real data is untouched.
